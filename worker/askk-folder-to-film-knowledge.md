## What it is

- Folder to Film is a service from Eastern Shore AI, LLC (Fruitland, Maryland). The customer uploads the photos, videos, audio and files they already have, and we turn them into a narrated short film.
- It works for almost any story: a family trip, the funny story everyone tells, a retirement video for Mom or Dad, a send-off for a coworker who's leaving (what they accomplished, goodbyes from the team: "better than a goodbye cake"), a wedding (how they met, the proposal, the big day), a tribute or memorial, milestones (anniversaries, graduations, a new baby, big birthdays), gifts, and histories of a family, church or business. It doesn't have to be serious.
- The example film on the page is a 100-year history of a small New Hampshire church (Christian Believers Fellowship). It's one example; most stories are smaller.

## Price and what's included

- $199.99 for the film, paid once. No subscription.
- Included: a film up to about 8 minutes, using up to about 75 photos and clips; a written script; narration; music; chapters; two rounds of changes (a new version after each); a private share page and a download.
- Longer stories, or churches and businesses with a lot of material, are quoted up front.
- Sales tax: Maryland buyers pay Maryland's 6% sales tax on top (a film delivered electronically is a taxable digital product in Maryland). Buyers outside Maryland pay no sales tax. Checkout shows the exact total before paying.
- Promotion codes can be entered at checkout when we have one.

## How it works (the steps)

1. **Pay once.** The "Start my film · $199.99" button goes straight to a secure card checkout page. There's no form to fill out first.
2. **Your dashboard opens right away.** After paying, the customer lands on their own private dashboard (and gets an email with the link). There they name the project, say whose story it is and when they need it, and answer 12 quick questions (tone, style, narrator, music, length, and so on). Any question can be left as "Experts pick."
3. **Upload everything.** Any type of file: photos (iPhone photos and Live Photos included), videos from phones, camcorders or GoPros, audio files and voice memos, scans, PDFs, Word documents, slideshows, spreadsheets, screenshots of texts or emails, and zip files. Whole folders can be uploaded at once. Big videos are fine (up to 50 GB per file). Links to Google Photos, iCloud, Dropbox, Facebook or YouTube can go in the links box. No sorting, labeling, renaming or converting needed. Paper photos: snap them with a phone or scan them; phone snapshots are fine, and we straighten, crop and clean them up. Uploads can happen over as many days as needed.
4. **Press Start production.** When everything is in, the customer presses Start production on the dashboard. We email them right away with the date their demo will be ready: 2 to 3 business days (weekends and major holidays don't count). After that, uploads close and the details lock; anything forgotten can be emailed by replying to any of our emails.
5. **Watch the demo.** The demo appears on the dashboard. The customer tells us what to change there (names, dates, pronunciations, a photo they just found) or presses Approve.
6. **Final film, the next business day** after their changes. It plays on the dashboard, can be shared with a link, and can be downloaded to keep. We can also post it to YouTube as private, unlisted (link only) or public, their choice.

## Styles and choices

- Film styles: Documentary (slow, Ken Burns-style moves over photos shown as framed prints), Animated cartoon (playful, slapstick), Storybook, Movie trailer, News report, Scrapbook, or Experts pick.
- Tone: heartfelt, funny, adventure, reverent, or Experts pick.
- Narrator: older or younger man or woman (AI voices), or the customer records it themselves and we build around their voice. We check how names are said.
- Music: hymns, piano, folk, orchestral, upbeat, music from their era, or Experts pick. We use licensed or original music; we can't put copyrighted pop songs into a film that may be shared online.
- Moments with no photos: we can add illustrations (soft watercolor, or cartoon for funny stories), one for each moment the narrator describes, matched to the people and place. Real photos always come first.
- Maps: for stories tied to a place, the film can open on a map and move into the town; for a trip, the map follows the route.

The finished-film email links to it. It's a preview: they can still change any photo, caption or word, and nothing is printed until they approve a final proof. Before the film is finished, the keepsakes page shows sample previews made from their uploaded photos instead.

Family copies ($79 each) are reprints of the hardcover, so they open up once the hardcover ($149, which includes designing the book) is ordered; the hardcover is ordered once per film. For one copy, they enter the address when they pay; for two or more, they enter each person's name and U.S. address on the keepsakes page before paying. Relatives can also order their own: the buyer adds them under Manage Who Has Access with "Order keepsakes only", and they get an invitation to the keepsakes page (not the dashboard), sign in with an emailed code, and pay for their own copy (or the hardcover, if nobody has ordered it yet) or the audiobook. Their receipt and updates (like the book proof for a hardcover they ordered) come to them by email.

## AI and privacy

- We use AI and say where: narrators are AI voices unless the customer records it; illustrations are AI-generated; AI helps sort materials and draft the script. A person checks every name, date and picture, and nothing is final until the customer approves it.
- Uploaded files travel over an encrypted connection and are stored privately and encrypted on secure servers (Cloudflare, if someone asks who), for that project only. Inside Eastern Shore AI, only the person making the film sees them.
- The dashboard needs more than the link: each person signs in with a 6-digit code emailed to them, and only the buyer and the people the buyer adds can get one. The buyer chooses what each added person can do (add photos and chat; also change the details and answers; or order keepsakes only) and can change or remove anyone at any time.
- We never sell customers' files or information, never use them in our marketing without written permission, and never use them to train AI; we turn off AI training in our tools wherever they allow it. Cards are handled by our secure payment processor; we never see the card number.
- The finished film's share page plays it for anyone with the link and is kept out of search engines. YouTube only if the customer asks, with the visibility they choose.
- The finished film and dashboard are kept as long as the customer wants; the original uploads are deleted 12 months after the final film, or sooner on request. Full details: the privacy notice at easternshore.ai/folder-to-film-terms.html#privacy.
- The customer owns the finished film and keeps their own photos and materials. They need to have the right to share what they send.

## Rules and refunds

- Customers must be 18 or older to order; they confirm it at checkout.
- We don't make: anything illegal; sexual or adult content; political advertising or campaign content (made to support or oppose a candidate, party, ballot question or law, or to raise money for one; a family film about someone who held public office is fine); hate, harassment or threats; false or harmful claims about real people; deceptive fakes of real people; material the customer doesn't have the rights to.
- If a project breaks the terms, we stop at any stage and refund what was paid minus the card processing fee (the payment processor keeps it on refunds; about $6). Sales tax is refunded in full.
- Keepsakes (the hardcover, copies and the audiobook) are made to order and can't be canceled or refunded once paid. A book that arrives damaged, misprinted or lost in the mail is replaced free if they tell us within 30 days of when it should have arrived. A mistake in a proof they approved isn't a misprint, but we'll fix it in a new order. If we can't make or deliver a keepsake, a full refund.
- Changing their mind: before the customer presses Start production, they can cancel and get what they paid back minus the card processing fee (about $6). After that, payments aren't refundable, but two rounds of changes are included. If we can't deliver, a full refund.
- Full terms: easternshore.ai/folder-to-film-terms.html

## Using your dashboard (for customers who are signed in)

- **Getting in:** the dashboard link from the payment email opens it. The browser they paid from is signed in automatically; on any other phone or computer we email a 6-digit code to the buyer's address or to someone they added. The code lasts 15 minutes.
- **Quick links** at the top jump to each part: Upload Photos and Files, Answer the Questions, Start Production, Talk With Us (the project chat), Add Notes, Keepsakes and Manage Who Has Access.
- **Step 1, About your film:** a name for the project, whose story it is and what it's about, the occasion and the date they need it by (all optional except the name and the story). The story box takes as much as they want to write: a biography, a church history, an obituary or family notes they already have can be pasted in, or uploaded as a file with the other files in Step 2. It saves as they type.
- **Step 2, Upload:** "Click to add photos & videos" (on phones it opens the camera roll), "Click to add any file" (Files, iCloud, Google Drive, documents, audio, zips) and, on computers, "Click to add a whole folder", or drag files into the box. Any file type, any size; big videos are fine. Keep the page open (and the phone unlocked) until the uploads finish; they can add more over as many days as they like. A file that's already uploaded is skipped, and each file shows who added it. The × removes a file.
- **Step 3, 12 quick questions:** tap an answer or skip it; skipped ones are "Experts pick". "See an example of each" under the film style question shows the six styles with a picture of each.
- **Notes:** spellings, nicknames, people or photos to leave out, the feeling they want. Saves as they type.
- **Step 4, Start production:** pressed when everything is in. It needs a project name, a few words about the story, at least one file or link, and no uploads still running. After it, the details, uploads, answers and notes lock, and anything new goes through the project chat (we can reopen uploads if they have more files). We email them the demo date right away.
- **Timing:** the demo is usually ready 2 to 3 business days after Start production (weekends and major holidays don't count); the final film usually comes the next business day after they send changes or approve.
- **Demo:** it plays right on the dashboard. They type their changes and press "Send my changes", or "Approve as is". Two rounds of changes are included; a round is all their notes on one version of the demo.
- **Final film:** plays on the dashboard with "Download to keep" and "Copy a link to share". The share link plays the film for anyone they send it to, without opening the dashboard.
- **Project chat ("Talk with the Eastern Shore AI team"):** for questions and anything about their film; Chris, who is making the film, replies, usually within an hour during the day, and we email them when we reply. Once production starts, the chat moves to the top of the dashboard.
- **Manage Who Has Access:** the buyer adds family, friends or coworkers by email and picks what each can do: "Add photos and chat" (the default: they can upload their own files, remove only files they uploaded, read the details and answers, and chat, but not change what the buyer wrote), "Add photos, chat, and change the details and answers" (also edits the details, answers and notes), or "Order keepsakes only" (they get the keepsakes page, not the dashboard). Only the buyer can press Start production, approve the demo or ask for changes, and add people. Each person signs in with an emailed code. The buyer can change anyone's access or remove them at any time; removing signs them out right away.
- **Keepsakes:** the Keepsakes link at the top of the dashboard (and, once production starts, the Keepsakes box beside the dashboard) opens a keepsakes page made from their project: once the film is finished, it shows their real book to page through; before that, sample previews built from their own photos. They buy the hardcover, copies and the audiobook right there, by card. Printed books ship free to U.S. addresses about 2 to 3 weeks after they approve a proof.
- **Need Help?** (this assistant, bottom right of the dashboard) answers questions about the process. It can't see their files or chat, change anything, or take payments.
