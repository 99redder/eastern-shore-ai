Read-only grounding for Ask K on folder-to-film.html. Keep it in step with the page (folder-to-film.html), the customer dashboard (my-film.html) and src/folder-to-film.js. If something isn't here, Ask K should say it isn't sure and offer contact@easternshore.ai or (302) 907-9162.

## What it is

- Folder to Film is a service from Eastern Shore AI, LLC (Fruitland, Maryland). The customer uploads the photos, videos, audio and files they already have, and we turn them into a narrated short film.
- It works for almost any story: a family trip, the funny story everyone tells, a retirement video for Mom or Dad, a send-off for a coworker who's leaving (what they accomplished, goodbyes from the team: "better than a goodbye cake"), a wedding (how they met, the proposal, the big day), a tribute or memorial, milestones (anniversaries, graduations, a new baby, big birthdays), gifts, and histories of a family, church or business. It doesn't have to be serious.
- The example film on the page is a 100-year history of a small New Hampshire church (Christian Believers Fellowship). It's one example; most stories are smaller.

## Price and what's included

- Included: a film up to about 8 minutes, using up to about 75 photos and clips; a written script; narration; music; chapters; two rounds of changes (a new version after each); a private share page and a download.
- Longer stories, or churches and businesses with a lot of material, are quoted up front.

## How it works (the steps)

1. **Pay once.** The "Start my film · $199.99" button goes straight to secure checkout by Stripe. There's no form to fill out first.
2. **Your dashboard opens right away.** After paying, the customer lands on their own private dashboard (and gets an email with the link). There they name the project, say whose story it is and when they need it, and answer 12 quick questions (tone, style, narrator, music, length, and so on). Any question can be left as "Experts pick."
3. **Upload everything.** Any type of file: photos (iPhone photos and Live Photos included), videos from phones, camcorders or GoPros, audio files and voice memos, scans, PDFs, Word documents, slideshows, spreadsheets, screenshots of texts or emails, and zip files. Whole folders can be uploaded at once. Big videos are fine (up to 50 GB per file). Links to Google Photos, iCloud, Dropbox, Facebook or YouTube can go in the links box. No sorting, labeling, renaming or converting needed. Paper photos: snap them with a phone or scan them; phone snapshots are fine, and we straighten, crop and clean them up. Uploads can happen over as many days as needed.
4. **Press Start production.** When everything is in, the customer presses Start production on the dashboard. We email them right away with the date their demo will be ready: 2 to 3 business days (weekends and major holidays don't count). After that, uploads close and the details lock; anything forgotten can be emailed by replying to any of our emails.
5. **Watch the demo.** The demo appears on the dashboard. The customer tells us what to change there (names, dates, pronunciations, a photo they just found) or presses Approve.
6. **Final film, the next business day** after their changes. It plays on the dashboard, can be shared with a link, and can be downloaded to keep. We can also post it to YouTube as private, unlisted (link only) or public, their choice.

## Styles and choices

- Film styles: Documentary (slow, Ken Burns-style moves over photos shown as framed prints), Animated cartoon (playful, slapstick), Storybook, Movie trailer, News report, Scrapbook, or Experts pick.
- Tone: heartfelt, funny, adventure, reverent, or Experts pick.
- Narrator: older or younger man or woman (AI voices), or the customer records it themselves and we build around their voice. We check how names are said.
- Music: hymns, piano, folk, orchestral, upbeat, music from their era, or Experts pick. We use licensed or original music; we can't put copyrighted pop songs into a film that may be shared online.
- Moments with no photos: we can add illustrations (soft watercolor, or cartoon for funny stories), one for each moment the narrator describes, matched to the people and place. Real photos always come first.
- Maps: for stories tied to a place, the film can open on a map and move into the town; for a trip, the map follows the route.

## AI and privacy

- We use AI and say where: narrators are AI voices unless the customer records it; illustrations are AI-generated; AI helps sort materials and draft the script. A person checks every name, date and picture, and nothing is final until the customer approves it.
- Uploaded files are stored privately for that project only. The share page is private by default and kept out of search engines; only people with the link can see it.
- The customer owns the finished film and keeps their own photos and materials. They need to have the right to share what they send.

## Rules and refunds

- Customers must be 18 or older to order; they confirm it at checkout.
- We don't make: anything illegal; sexual or adult content; political advertising or campaign content (made to support or oppose a candidate, party, ballot question or law, or to raise money for one; a family film about someone who held public office is fine); hate, harassment or threats; false or harmful claims about real people; deceptive fakes of real people; material the customer doesn't have the rights to.
- If a project breaks the terms, we stop at any stage and refund what was paid minus Stripe's processing fee (Stripe keeps it on refunds; about $6 on a card payment). Sales tax is refunded in full.
- Changing their mind: before the customer presses Start production, they can cancel and get what they paid back minus Stripe's processing fee (about $6 on a card). After that, payments aren't refundable, but two rounds of changes are included. If we can't deliver, a full refund.
