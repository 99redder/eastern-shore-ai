Do This Now Upon Receipt

Thanks for your purchase. This quick-start insert will help you prepare your Survival Node for long-term readiness.

Complete these 3 steps first

Your Survival Node phone and solar battery arrive in their original manufacturer packaging inside the hard case — both have been opened, inspected, tested, and repacked before shipment. The two Faraday bags are laid flat in the case, separated from the other components. The phone is intentionally shipped at a reduced battery level (below 30%) in accordance with lithium battery shipping guidelines. Complete the steps below, then place both devices in their Faraday bags before sealing for long-term storage.

  1. Charge the solar battery. Plug your solar battery into wall power using the included charger. You can charge it to 100% to verify all LEDs are solid and it is working correctly, then bring it back down to 50–70% before sealing it for long-term storage.
  2. Charge the phone. Plug the phone in using the included wall charger and USB-C cable. Charge it to 50–60%. This is the ideal range for long-term lithium battery storage. Do not store it fully charged or fully drained.
  3. Seal it for readiness. Place the phone and power hub into their Faraday bags, fold the top twice, seal them tightly, then store both in the case with the included reusable silica gel pack.

Storage conditions

Store your Survival Node in a cool, dry location — ideally between 60°F (15°C) and 85°F (30°C). Avoid cars, attics, and garages where temperatures reach damaging extremes.

The included silica gel pack is reusable. Recharge it when the blue indicator dot turns light pink.

  • Recharge method: place the pack with the indicator dot facing up and microwave it for 3–5 minutes.
  • Important: do not exceed 210°F equivalent heating conditions, and check the pack as it recharges.
  • Ready to reuse: once the indicator dot turns blue again, the pack can be reused.

6-month maintenance check

Every 6 months, open the case and run this quick readiness check:

  1. Open the case and remove the phone, solar battery, and silica gel pack.
  2. Inspect the phone and battery for swelling, deformation, corrosion, or moisture damage.
  3. Check the phone battery level. If needed, charge the phone back to roughly 50–60%.
  4. Check the solar battery level. If needed, charge it to 50–70%.
  5. Power the phone on briefly to confirm it still boots normally and the core apps are accessible.
  6. Inspect the charging cable, wall charger, Faraday bags, and case hardware for wear or damage.
  7. Recharge or replace the silica gel pack if the blue indicator dot has turned pink. Recharge directions are listed above in the Storage conditions section of this insert.
  8. Power the phone fully back down, reseal both devices in their Faraday bags, and return everything to the case.

The full Users Guide on the phone includes more detailed maintenance and battery-care guidance.
