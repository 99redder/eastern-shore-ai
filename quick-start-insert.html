Survival Node

Do This Now Upon Receipt

A quick-start insert to help you prepare your Survival Node for long-term readiness.

Complete these 3 steps first

  1. Charge the solar battery. Plug your solar battery into wall power using the included charger. You can charge it to 100% to verify all LEDs are solid and it is working correctly, then bring it back down to 50–70% before sealing it for long-term storage.
  2. Charge the phone. Plug the phone in using the included wall charger and USB-C cable. Charge it to 50–60%. This is the ideal range for long-term lithium battery storage. Do not store it fully charged or fully drained.
  3. Seal it for readiness. Place the phone and power hub into their Faraday bags, fold the top twice, seal them tightly, then store both in the case with the included reusable silica gel packs.

Storage conditions

Store your Survival Node in a cool, dry location — ideally between 60°F (15°C) and 85°F (30°C). Avoid cars, attics, and garages where temperatures reach damaging extremes.

The included silica gel packs are reusable. When the orange indicator beads turn dark green, they are saturated and need to be reactivated.

  • Preferred method: lay the packs flat in direct sunlight for 1–2 days.
  • Alternative: microwave for about 5 minutes (start with less; different microwaves vary in power).
  • Alternative: oven at 200–250°F for 30 minutes to 2 hours.

SIM card & cellular note

No SIM card or service plan is required. All core features — AI, maps, and reference apps — work fully offline.

We do not recommend attempting to connect this phone to a cellular network or installing a SIM card.

Where to find the full Users Guide

The full Survival Node Users Guide is pre-loaded on the phone.

Open the Files folder on the home screen to view it offline any time.

Tip: After you complete these steps, store the case in a dry, temperature-stable location and set a reminder for your 6-month maintenance check.
