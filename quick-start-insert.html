Survival Node · Quick Start

Do This Now Upon Receipt

Thanks for your purchase. A few steps now prepare your Survival Node for long-term readiness.

Your phone arrives in protective bubble wrap inside the hard case; the solar battery is in its original packaging (opened, inspected, tested, repacked). The two Mission Darkness Faraday bags are in their original packaging, placed under the hard case in the shipping box — not inside it. The phone ships below 30% charge per lithium-battery shipping rules. Complete the steps below, then seal both devices in their Faraday bags for storage.

Complete these 4 steps first

  1. Check the solar battery. Press the battery's power button to read the charge indicator. If 2 or more of the 4 blue LEDs are lit, it's ready for storage — no charging needed. If fewer than 2 are lit, plug it into wall power with the included charger and bring it to at least the 2-LED level (50%) before storage.
  2. Charge the phone. Plug the phone in with the included wall charger and USB-C cable and charge it to 50–60% — the ideal range for long-term lithium battery storage. Do not store it fully charged or fully drained.
  3. Set up your desiccant. Remove and discard the small temporary white desiccant bag (a shipping buffer only). Then open the premium long-term desiccant pouch from its sealed plastic bag and place it inside the case — this is your permanent moisture protection.
  4. Seal it for readiness. Place the phone and solar battery into their Faraday bags, fold the top over twice, seal tightly, then store both in the case alongside the premium desiccant pouch.

Storage Conditions

Store in a cool, dry location — ideally 60–85°F (15–30°C). Avoid cars, attics, and garages where temperatures reach damaging extremes.

The reusable silica gel pack should be recharged when its blue indicator dot turns light pink:

  • Recharge: place the pack indicator-dot-up and microwave for 3–5 minutes.
  • Caution: do not exceed 210°F heating, and watch it while it recharges.
  • Reuse: once the dot turns blue again, the pack is ready.

6-Month Maintenance Check

Every 6 months, open the case and run a quick readiness check:

  1. Remove the phone, solar battery, and silica gel pack.
  2. Inspect both for swelling, deformation, corrosion, or moisture.
  3. Charge the phone back to roughly 50–60% if needed.
  4. Check the solar battery LEDs; charge to 2–3 of 4 blue LEDs (50–75%) if needed.
  5. Power the phone on briefly to confirm it boots and core apps open.
  6. Inspect the cable, charger, Faraday bags, and case hardware for wear.
  7. Recharge or replace the silica pack if its dot has turned pink (see Storage Conditions).
  8. Power fully down, reseal both devices in their Faraday bags, return to the case.
