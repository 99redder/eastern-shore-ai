Your phone was intentionally drained to ~30% before shipping to comply with lithium battery regulations. This is normal.
  1. Charge the solar battery — plug into wall power. Charge to 100% (all 4 blue LEDs solid) to verify it's working, then bring back down to 2–3 LEDs (50–75%) for storage.
  2. Charge the phone — use the included wall charger and USB-C cable. Charge to 50–60% — ideal for long-term storage. Do not store at full charge or fully drained.
  3. Seal for readiness — place phone and solar battery into their Faraday bags, fold the top twice, seal tightly, store both in the case with silica gel packs.

Store in a cool, dry location — ideally between 60°F (15°C) and 85°F (30°C). Avoid cars, attics, and garages where temperatures reach damaging extremes. Store the case upright, away from direct sunlight.

Silica Gel Packs

The included silica gel packs absorb moisture and are reusable. Recharge them when the blue indicator dot turns light pink. Place pack with indicator dot facing up and microwave for 3–5 minutes. Once the dot turns blue again, the pack is ready to reuse.

⚠️ Silica Pack Safety: Keep indicator side facing up, monitor while heating, do not exceed 210°F. Discard if damaged, leaking, or overheated.

☀️ POWER & SOLAR CHARGING

The solar panel is not designed for all-day normal use — it's designed for emergency conditions where you use the phone briefly and then power it off. Solar input accumulates while the phone is off.

💡 Eastern Shore AI founder Chris Gorham powered his test Survival Node exclusively via solar for over a year without wall power.

The Rule: Use It, Then Shut It Off

  • Place panel in direct overhead sun; re-angle as sunlight shifts
  • Power the phone off when not actively in use — most important habit
  • Use wall charging whenever available
  • Avoid high-heat charging sessions with phone connected
  • Plan your power budget: prioritize mission-critical tasks, then shut down
  • Do not charge above 113°F (45°C) — if hot to touch, stop charging and move to shade
  • Do not store below 14°F (−10°C) — allow to warm to room temperature before charging
  • Ideal storage: 60°F–75°F (15°C–24°C) at ~50% charge
🔴 If the phone back is warping outward or the power hub casing is deformed — stop using immediately. A swollen lithium battery is a fire and puncture hazard.

Do not charge it, compress it, or dispose in regular trash. Take it to a certified battery recycling location — Best Buy, Home Depot, and many auto parts stores accept lithium batteries at no charge.

Manufacturer Safety Notices

  • Do not charge and discharge simultaneously
  • Keep product dry — rain, moisture, and liquids corrode electronics
  • Do not attempt to disassemble
🔋 BATTERY MANAGEMENT
  • Keep between 20–85% — less stress on cells. Use Battery Bot Pro to alarm at 85%
  • Avoid deep discharges — recharge before 15%
  • Charge at room temperature — charging hot or cold causes permanent loss
  • Use original or quality USB-C cable — cheap cables stress the battery controller
  1. Charge phone to 40–60% — ideal resting state
  2. Power the phone completely off
  3. Charge solar hub to 2–3 blue LEDs (50–75%)
  4. Store between 60°F–75°F (15°C–24°C)
  5. Check every 3–6 months; top off to 50% if needed
📌 A battery stored at 100% or 0% for months will lose permanent capacity. The 40–60% target is the most impactful habit for kit longevity.
🛡️ EMP PROTOCOL

A Faraday bag is a shielded enclosure lined with conductive material (TitanRF fabric) that blocks electromagnetic signals. Every Survival Node includes two Mission Darkness Faraday bags — one for the phone, one for the solar battery. Inside a properly sealed bag, electronics are shielded from:

  • Electromagnetic Pulse (EMP) events and nuclear burst EMP
  • Solar flares and geomagnetic storms (CMEs)
  • RF tracking, cellular location signals, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth
  • RFID skimming
⚠️ Faraday bags do NOT protect against physical damage, water immersion, or static discharge.

Sealing Protocol

  • At first sign of risk, isolate all mission-critical electronics immediately
  • Fold the opening over itself at least twice before sealing Velcro
  • Check for punctures, worn seams, or contaminated seal surfaces
  • Keep spare storage sleeves in the case as backup

🛠️ TROUBLESHOOTING
  1. Check battery level and charging cable integrity
  2. Check storage capacity and free space
  3. Check app permissions and recent setting changes
  4. Full reboot, then retry core apps

If behavior persists, document what changed right before failure — that usually reveals root cause faster than random toggling.

Common Issues

  • AI giving wrong answers? Start a fresh chat session, rephrase your question, or try a larger model. Models can hallucinate.
  • Device feels hot? Stop charging immediately, move to shade. If phone back is warped, see Swollen Battery Warning.
  • App crashing? Apps requiring Google Play Services may not work on debloated OS — this is expected. Core offline apps are not affected.
  • Phone won't power on? Connect to solar hub and charge for at least 15 minutes before trying again.

Contact support@easternshore.ai if a core app fails to function.
