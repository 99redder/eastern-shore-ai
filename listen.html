Listen offline or in the car

Add it to a podcast app on your phone. It downloads there, plays without internet and in the car (CarPlay or Android Auto), and remembers where you stopped.

Add to Apple Podcasts
On iPhone, if Apple Podcasts doesn't show it
  1. Press Copy the podcast link above.
  2. Open the Podcasts app and tap Library.
  3. Tap the ••• button at the top, then Follow a Show by URL.
  4. Paste the link and tap Follow. The audiobook shows up in your Library.
On Android
  1. Press Copy the podcast link above.
  2. Open a podcast app such as Pocket Casts or AntennaPod (free in the Play Store).
  3. Find Add podcast by URL (in Pocket Casts, search and paste the link) and paste it.

Keep the file

Download the MP3 to keep on your phone or computer, or to copy to a USB stick for the car.

Download the MP3
Put it on your home screen
  1. iPhone: tap the Share button (the square with an arrow) in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: tap the ⋮ menu in Chrome, then Add to Home screen.

Then it opens like an app, straight to this player.
