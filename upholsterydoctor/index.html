What we work on

The Upholstery Doctor is an established furniture reupholstery shop on the Eastern Shore. From the smallest project to a complete home overhaul, every piece gets the same careful, hands-on work.

Residential

Sofas, loveseats, dining chairs, wing chairs and bar stools. We rebuild what's worn and keep what's sturdy.

Commercial

Restaurant seating and other commercial work, built to stand up to daily use.

Antique restoration

Family heirlooms and older pieces restored with care, so they're ready for another generation.

Most people have never had a piece reupholstered. Here's what to expect.

  2. Pick a fabric
