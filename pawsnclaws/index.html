The stay

Their vacation, while you're on yours

The Provosts used to be a camping and traveling family, so they know finding the right boarding place is the hardest part of going away. Here's what every guest gets.

Heated & air conditioned

Climate-controlled kennels, comfortable in every season.

Covered outdoor runs

Every kennel has an attached run, covered from the hot sun and sudden storms.

Play time, 3 times a day

Individual, supervised play in the fenced yard, from 8 am to 9 pm.

Blankets for everyone

Bring their favorite blankie, bed or toy too, if it helps them settle in.

Meals & snacks included

Built into their reasonable rates. Call for rates and availability.

Vet on call

Their own vet is on call for emergencies.

Siblings can bunk together

Large kennels are big enough for family members to share, if you ask.

Bathing & nail trims

Available during a stay, with a salon-quality line of shampoos.

Grooming Salon

Kiersten's grooming hut, right on the property

Paws N Claws Cottages Grooming Salon handles all phases of grooming, and Kiersten is committed to a safe, calm and relaxing visit. Boarding guests can be groomed during their stay when she has openings.

What Kiersten does

  • Basic baths
  • Nail trimming
  • Special cuts
  • Ear & teeth cleaning
  • De-shedding
  • A calm, relaxing visit

Grooming is booked separately through the salon: call Kiersten at (443) 944-7444. She takes PayPal, Venmo, Cash App and cash (no credit cards).

Before you come

What to bring to check-in

  • An up-to-date vaccination record, including rabies, DHLPP and Bordetella
  • A recent flea treatment
  • Their favorite blanket, bed or toy, if you like

Check-in and check-out times are in the visit section below.

Cats too

Cat suites inside

Cats have their own stacked suites with glass fronts. Call to ask about boarding your cat.
