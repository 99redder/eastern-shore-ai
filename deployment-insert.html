How to Deploy Your Kit

Getting your Survival Node running when you need it. No tech experience required — just follow the steps in order.

KEEP THIS CARD WITH YOUR CASE — it works even when the phone is dead or you're under stress.

Get it running

  1. Open the case and remove the gear. Unlock the padlock if you used one, flip the two latches, and lift the lid. Take the phone and the solar battery out of their Faraday bags — unfold the sealed top and slide each one out.
  2. Power on the phone. Press and hold the power button on the right side for 2–3 seconds until it vibrates and the screen lights up. Startup takes about 30–60 seconds. If it won't turn on, the battery is likely too low — do step 4 to charge it first, then come back and power it on.
  3. Turn on Airplane Mode immediately, and lower the brightness. Swipe down from the very top of the screen, tap the airplane icon (it highlights when on), then drag the brightness slider down. Keep Airplane Mode ON whenever you're not actively making a call or text — it's the single biggest battery saver, and every offline app still works.
  4. Connect power if you need it. Find the solar battery's attached USB-C cable (small oval connector) and plug it into the port on the bottom of the phone. It charges automatically — you can use the phone while it charges. Set the solar battery panel-side up in direct overhead sunlight and re-angle it as the sun moves. Don't set it on hot surfaces (asphalt, metal, car hoods).
  5. Golden rule — use it, then power it OFF. Do your task (look something up, check your location, ask the AI), then press and hold the power button and tap Power off. A fully powered-off phone uses zero battery. Brief, task-focused use followed by powering off is what lets the solar battery sustain the kit for weeks.

Battery quick-check. The phone's charge % is in the top-right corner of the screen. For the solar battery, press its side button: 4 blue LEDs = full, 2 = moderate (use conservatively), 1 = low, 0 / no lights = critically low — put the panel in direct sun immediately.

⚠ Hot-weather safety. While the solar battery charges in the sun, check it every 20–30 minutes — it should feel warm, not hot. If it's uncomfortably hot, move it to shade to cool before resuming. Never charge above 113°F (45°C). Also: do not charge the solar battery from a wall outlet and use it to charge the phone at the same time.

Using Your Kit

Everything works with no internet, Wi-Fi, or cell signal. The apps are already installed and ready.

The apps

Ask the AI (PocketPal)

Tap PocketPal on the home screen. Select Qwen 3.5-4B as the primary model (or Qwen 2.5-3B for faster answers). Tap the ↑ beside the message box → Pals tab → "Survival Node" to load the survival assistant. Type or speak your question. A long Qwen 3.5 answer can take 2–3 minutes — that's normal. Ask about first aid, water purification, shelter, navigation, or repairs. Always verify medical or safety-critical answers against WikiMed or the Survival Manual before acting.

Navigate (Organic Maps)

The entire US is pre-loaded for offline GPS. Open Organic Maps; your location is the blue dot (wait 30–60 sec for GPS lock). Tap the search bar, enter a place, then Directions → Start. GPS works in Airplane Mode and in a full blackout.

Medical & survival references (Kiwix, Survival Manual)

Open Kiwix → WikiMed for the full offline medical encyclopedia — search a symptom, injury, or medication. The Survival Manual app covers shelter, fire, water, food, first aid, and navigation (US Army field manual), fully offline.

Communications (if needed)

Briar sends text messages to another Briar user over a direct Bluetooth/Wi-Fi link — no signal, internet, or server. Both people must be nearby; use Add Contact to pair the first time. SOS Flashlight: tap SOS and the flashlight flashes the international distress signal automatically. Morse: type a message to flash or play it in Morse code for a mirror, light, or radio.

Manage power & put it away

  • Low phone battery (under 15%): connect it to the solar battery and keep the screen off until it reaches at least 30–40% before using again. Never drain repeatedly to 0%.
  • Low solar battery: place it panel-up in the brightest direct sun and give it several hours before charging the phone — solar charging is slow.
  • Between uses: power the phone fully off, slide the phone and solar battery into their Faraday bags, fold each top over at least twice and seal flat with no gaps, then return both to the case, latch it, and re-apply the padlock. A loose Faraday bag gives little EMP protection.
