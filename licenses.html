Open-Source Licenses

Most of the apps on the Survival Node phone, and its AI models and voices, are free and open-source software by other authors; it also carries reference content made by other people. Eastern Shore AI, LLC did not write them and has no affiliation with their authors. Here is each one with its license and where its source can be found. The same list and the full license texts are on the phone in File Manager → Download → Licenses. Kits prepared before November 17, 2026 may have a slightly different set of apps (see Kit Changes in the Terms of Sale).

Source code offer

Several apps below are licensed under the GNU General Public License (GPL). Their complete source code is published at the links listed. For at least three years after we ship your Survival Node, we will also send the corresponding source code for any GPL app on it to anyone who asks, for no more than our cost of sending it: email support@easternshore.ai.
