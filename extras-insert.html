Free Extras We Recommend Downloading

These excellent free libraries can't come preloaded on a kit we sell: their makers allow free personal use, not resale. So you add them yourself. It takes one evening at home on Wi-Fi, and then they work offline like everything else.

Do this at home on Wi-Fi now, before you ever need it.

All six together are about 12 GB. Your phone has room for them.

How to add them (about 10 minutes of tapping, then let it download)

  1. Plug the phone in and connect to Wi-Fi. Swipe down from the top of the screen and tap Wi-Fi.
  2. Open Kiwix and tap Download at the bottom right. That's Kiwix's free online library.
  3. Tap the magnifying glass at the top, type a name from the table above, then tap it in the list to download it. Do the same for each one; Kiwix downloads them one after another.
  4. Leave the phone plugged in on Wi-Fi until they're all done. Large ones can take an hour or more.
  5. Turn Wi-Fi back off. The new libraries now show up in Kiwix next to WikiMed, and work with no internet.
