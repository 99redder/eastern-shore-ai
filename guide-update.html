Before you start

  • Your Survival Node phone, charged to at least 30%.
  • Wi-Fi with working internet, such as your home Wi-Fi. No SIM card is needed.
  • About 5 minutes. Updating changes only the User Guide app. Your maps, AI models, libraries, photos and files stay exactly as they are.

The update, step by step

Do these on the Survival Node phone, in order.

1

Connect to Wi-Fi

  1. Swipe down from the top of the screen two times.
  2. Tap Internet.
  3. Turn Wi-Fi on, then tap your home network. Type the Wi-Fi password if it asks for one.

Airplane mode can stay on. Wi-Fi still works with it on.

2

Open the User Guide and check its version

  1. Open the User Guide app on the home screen.
  2. Scroll all the way to the bottom.
  3. The last line shows the version, such as Survival Node User Guide · v1.13.
It says v1.15You already have the newest guide. You're done! Turn Wi-Fi off (step 8).
It says any other version numberGo on to step 3.
No version number, or it says Gen 1You have a Gen 1 kit. Don't install this update: it's written for Gen 2 hardware, and your guide is the right one for your kit. Questions? Call or text (302) 907-9162.
3

Tap Update in the orange box

Scroll back to the top of the guide. Within a few seconds an orange box appears that says Guide update available. Tap the Update button in it.

Nothing seems to happen. That's normal: the download takes a few seconds and runs in the background.

No orange box after 30 seconds? Use the web browser method below instead.
4

Open the downloaded file

  1. Swipe down from the top of the screen.
  2. Tap the notification for the download. Its name ends in .apk, such as survival-node-user-guide-v1.15.apk.

Don't see it? Open File Manager, open the Download folder and tap the file with the newest version number.

5

Allow the install (first time only)

Android may say your phone isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source. It asks because the guide comes from us, not from an app store.

  1. Tap Settings.
  2. Turn on Allow from this source.
  3. Tap the back arrow ← at the top left.

If you've updated before, this screen may not appear. Go straight to step 6.

6

Tap Update, then Open

  1. Android asks "Do you want to update this app?" Tap Update.
  2. Wait a few seconds until it says the app is installed.
  3. Tap Open.
7

Check that it worked

Scroll to the bottom of the guide. It should now say v1.15, and the orange box is gone. You can close the notification for the download, too.

8

Turn Wi-Fi off and put the phone away

  1. Swipe down from the top two times, tap Internet and turn Wi-Fi off.
  2. Going back into storage? Charge the phone to about 60%, power it off and seal it in its Faraday bag, as the guide's storage section explains.

While you're on Wi-Fi, the guide's Maintenance section shows how to update your maps and other apps, too.

No orange box? Use the web browser

If the orange box never shows up but your guide isn't on v1.15, or tapping Update didn't download anything, get the update from this page instead.

  1. Keep Wi-Fi on (step 1).
  2. Open Fennec on the home screen.
  3. Tap the address bar at the top, type this, then tap the arrow or Enter key on the keyboard:easternshore.ai/guide-update
  4. This page opens. Scroll down to this section and tap the orange button:

Version 1.15 · about 1 MB · for the Survival Node phone only

The download button appears here when you open this page on the Survival Node phone.

  1. When Fennec says the download is finished, tap Open. You can also swipe down from the top and tap the download.
  2. Then do steps 5 to 8 above. Fennec may ask for permission the first time (step 5), even if you've updated before.

If something goes wrong

The guide says "Webpage not available"

On Wi-Fi: that Wi-Fi has no working internet. Turn Wi-Fi off and open the guide again; it opens normally. Try the update another time, or on another Wi-Fi you trust.

With no signal at all: you have version 1.4, 1.5 or 1.6, which can't open offline. This update fixes that. Connect to Wi-Fi (step 1) and the guide will open, then follow the steps.

I tapped Update and nothing downloaded

Wait 30 seconds, then swipe down from the top and look for the download. Still nothing? Use the web browser method.

It says "App not installed" or "There was a problem parsing the package"

The download didn't finish. Open File Manager, open the Download folder, press and hold the guide file you just downloaded and delete it. Then start again from step 3. If it happens again, call us.

A "Google Play Protect" box appeared

Some phones sold before November 2026 still have Google Play services, which checks apps that don't come from the Play Store. Tap More details if you see it, then Install anyway. If it asks whether to scan or send the app, either choice is fine.

It asks to "install" instead of "update"

Stop and call us at (302) 907-9162. That means the phone doesn't have the guide this update replaces, and we'll sort it out with you.

Will I lose anything?

No. Only the User Guide app is replaced. Your maps, AI models and chats, Kiwix libraries, photos and other files aren't touched.

Is it safe to install?

Yes. The file comes straight from easternshore.ai, and Android only lets it replace your guide because it carries the same digital signature as the guide we put on your phone.

Do I have to update?

No, the guide you have keeps working. The newest version has the most current instructions and terms, so we recommend it.

I don't have Wi-Fi at home

Use the Wi-Fi of a friend or family member you trust. Avoid public Wi-Fi, like coffee shops and hotels. You only need it for these few minutes.

What about the PDF guide in the Download folder?

The PDF is a dated copy and doesn't update. The User Guide app is the current version, so use it for the latest instructions and terms.
