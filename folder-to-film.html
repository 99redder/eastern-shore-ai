Questions

Good to know

What can I send?

Anything that helps tell the story, in any file type: photos (iPhone photos and Live Photos included), videos from phones, camcorders or GoPros, audio files and voice memos, scans, PDFs, Word documents, slideshows, spreadsheets, screenshots of texts or emails, and zipped folders. Links to Google Photos, iCloud, Dropbox, Facebook or YouTube work too. Large videos are fine. You don't need to sort, label, rename or convert anything, and if we ever can't open a file, we'll tell you. Please send only things you own or have permission to use.

Does it have to be serious?

Not at all. A road trip, a family legend that gets funnier every year, a wedding, a retirement roast, a coworker's send-off, the dog's greatest hits: pick Funny or Adventure and we write it that way, with music and a narrator to match. The church history on this page is one example.

Can I talk to a real person?

Yes. Your film dashboard has a project chat with Chris, the person making your film. We usually reply within an hour during the day and email you when we do. You can also call or text (302) 907-9162. Ask K, the assistant on this page, is for quick questions before you order.

Our old photos are prints. Is that OK?

Yes. Take a picture of each one with your phone, or scan them if you have a scanner; phone snapshots are fine. We straighten, crop and clean them up. The church's oldest pictures reached us as phone shots and a sideways scan in a PDF.

We don't have photos from the early years.

That's common. We add watercolor illustrations for the moments nobody photographed, in one consistent style, and if a real photo turns up later we swap it in.

How long is the video?

Up to about 8 minutes, using up to 75 photos and clips. Longer stories, or churches and businesses with a lot of material, get a quote up front.

How fast can I have it?

Your demo is ready 2–3 business days after you upload, and the final version the next business day after your changes. Need it for a memorial service this week? Ask about rush delivery.

Who can see it, and what happens to my photos?

Only people you send the link to. Your files are stored privately for your project only and never sold or used in our marketing. Your finished film and dashboard stay up for as long as you want them; the original uploads are deleted 12 months after your final film, or sooner if you ask. If you'd like the film on YouTube, we'll post it as private, unlisted or public. The details are in our privacy notice.

Do you use AI?

Yes, and we'll always tell you where. Narrators are AI voices unless you record it yourself, and the illustrations are AI-generated watercolors. AI also helps us sort your materials and draft the script. A person checks every name, date and picture, and nothing is final until you approve it.

What if something's wrong in the demo?

Tell us. Two rounds of changes are included: after each one we send you a new version, and the final arrives the next business day after you approve. More rounds are available if the story keeps growing.

Who owns the finished film?

It's yours to keep, share and show. Your photos and materials stay yours too; you just need to have the right to share what you send us.
