Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This page explains what information Eastern Shore AI, LLC collects through this website, our Survival Node checkout, our support tools, and the web design and AI services we provide; what we do with it; who helps us handle it; and how to reach us about it.

The short version

  • We collect what you give us through forms, checkout and chat, plus basic technical information from your browser.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • We may text you about your order from (302) 907-9162. These texts are not marketing. Reply STOP to stop them.
  • Google Analytics and the Reddit Pixel measure visits and ad results, and they use cookies.
  • Ask K's answers are written by an AI service. We don't save Ask K chats.
  • To correct or delete your information, email contact@easternshore.ai.

Who we are

Eastern Shore AI, LLC is a small business in Fruitland, Maryland. We build websites for local businesses, set up AI tools, and sell the Survival Node. This policy covers our pages at www.easternshore.ai and the orders, emails, texts and support that come from them. Purchases made through other sites, such as eBay, are covered by those sites' own privacy policies.

Information you give us

Contact, website request and quote forms

Our forms ask for your name and email address and, depending on the form, your phone number, business name, current website, preferred way and time to be contacted, the device or system you use, your order number, and your message.

When you send a form, our server emails it to our business inbox. Form messages are not saved in our database. The emails are sent through Cloudflare Email Sending, with Resend as a backup, and our inbox is hosted by Google (Gmail). We use what you send to answer you and follow up on the work you asked about.

Newsletter

Our newsletter sign-up forms send the email address you enter straight to Brevo, our email newsletter service, which keeps our newsletter list. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any newsletter email, or by asking us.

Survival Node purchases

Checkout takes place on a secure page run by Stripe. Stripe collects your name, email address, phone number, billing address, shipping address and payment card, and shares everything except your full card details with us so we can fill your order. Your card details go straight to Stripe: we never see or store your card number or security code. Stripe shows us only basic card information, such as the card brand and last four digits. We also record that you agreed to the Terms of Sale, along with the version and the time.

We keep your order in our order system, a database hosted by Cloudflare. It holds your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, order number, estimated ship date, tracking number and the battery test result for your phone. We use it to ship your order, email you about it (order confirmation, shipping, delivery and a request for a review), answer your questions, handle returns and refunds, and keep business and tax records.

To ship your order, we give your name, shipping address and email address to Pirate Ship, the service we use to buy shipping labels, and your package goes by USPS. Pirate Ship may email you tracking details when the label is created.

If you book our bring-your-own-gear setup service, we also collect the setup address and the date and time you choose.

Text messages

Checkout asks for your phone number. We may text that number about your order, such as to confirm your shipping address, tell you it has shipped or been delivered, or answer a question. These texts come from a person at Eastern Shore AI at (302) 907-9162 and are not marketing. Message and data rates may apply. To stop, reply STOP or tell us by phone or email, and we will stop texting you. Stopping texts does not affect your order.

We send these texts by hand using Google Voice, so Google handles those messages. We do not share your mobile number with anyone for their marketing.

Bookings, Website Care Plan, invoices and quotes

  • Setup and lesson bookings: when you book an OpenClaw setup or AI lessons, we collect your name, email address, phone number (optional), preferred contact method, and the date and time you pick. Payment is made through Stripe Checkout, and we keep the booking in our database.
  • Website Care Plan: when you sign the plan agreement online, we record your name, title and email address, the exact agreement text you signed, the time, and your IP address and browser details, as a record of your signature. Payments are handled by Stripe. If you choose automatic renewal, Stripe keeps your card on file and runs the yearly charge, and you can manage it through Stripe's billing page. The Find my plan page emails your plan link only to an address that matches a client.
  • Invoices and quotes: we use your name, email address, phone number, and the work and prices involved to prepare them. They are emailed to you, and invoices can be paid through a Stripe payment link.

Folder to Film story films

Folder to Film has its own privacy notice, because you share personal photos, videos and stories with us to make your film. In short: your files are used only to make your film, never sold or used in our marketing without your permission, stored privately for your project, and deleted 90 days after your final film. It also lists the AI services that help make the films. For Folder to Film, that notice applies where it differs from this policy.

Ask K and live support chat

Ask K is the AI assistant on the Survival Node page.

Ask K's answers are written by an AI service, Anthropic's Claude. To write each answer, your question, the recent messages in that chat, and basic page context (such as which part of the page you are viewing) are sent to Anthropic. A quick check that your question is about the Survival Node is also sent to Claude. Anthropic handles that information under its own terms and privacy policy. We don't save Ask K chats on our servers; we keep only daily counts, such as how many questions were answered. Please don't type passwords, payment details or other sensitive information into the chat.

To prevent abuse, Ask K uses Cloudflare Turnstile, a security check that may look at browser and device signals, and we count requests from each IP address for a short time to limit how many can be sent.

If you ask Ask K for an order update, the email address (and optional order number) you enter is used only to look up Survival Node orders placed with that address on our website and to email their status to that same address. Order details are never shown in the chat or sent to the AI provider. We don't store the address you enter; a one-way code made from it is kept for up to a day so we can limit how many updates are sent to one address.

If you choose Talk to a human, that conversation is stored in our database, hosted by Cloudflare, so our team can reply and follow up. Your recent Ask K messages are copied into it so we can see what you already asked. Our team is notified through Discord, and that notification includes the page you were on and a short excerpt of the conversation. Support chats are kept until we delete them.

Analytics, ads, cookies and other services on our pages

  • Google Analytics: most of our pages use Google Analytics to count visits and see how people find and use the site. It records things like the pages you view, the site or ad that sent you, your device and browser, and your approximate location, and it uses cookies. When a Survival Node order is completed, the page also sends Google Analytics the order total and the Stripe order reference (not your name, email or address) so we can see which visits lead to sales.
  • Reddit Pixel: most of our pages load the Reddit Pixel, which tells Reddit when someone visits a page, views the Survival Node, or completes a purchase (with the order total and order reference), so we can measure our Reddit ads. Reddit uses cookies and information from your browser to do this.
  • Our own visit count: the Survival Node page sends our server a short note on each visit. We store the date, the site or ad that sent you, your device type (phone, tablet or computer), the town, region and country your internet connection points to, and a one-way code made from your IP address and browser. The code lets us count unique visitors without keeping your IP address. Our team sees these counts in a daily summary.
  • YouTube videos: the Survival Node and Testing pages show videos from YouTube. When a video player loads, YouTube (Google) may set cookies and collect information under Google's privacy policy.
  • Hosting and fonts: our pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare, which see your IP address and browser details to serve pages and protect the site. Most pages use fonts stored on our own site; a few load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google.
  • Stripe and Turnstile: Stripe's checkout page and Cloudflare Turnstile (Ask K's security check) may use their own cookies and device signals to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Our own browser storage: we don't set our own cookies. After a Survival Node checkout, the page saves a small note in your browser so the same purchase isn't reported to analytics twice.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use a browser extension that blocks trackers. Most of the site works without analytics and ad cookies; Ask K needs the Turnstile check to work.

Security and abuse prevention

To stop spam and abuse, our forms and chat count requests from each IP address for a short time (up to a day) to limit how many can be sent, and some forms have hidden fields that catch bots. Our servers keep technical logs for about a week so we can find and fix problems. Our pages and forms use encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and our admin tools require a password and a sign-in code.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't share your contact details with anyone for their own marketing. We share information only with the services that help us run the business, and only what each one needs:

  • Stripe: payments, saved cards for automatic renewals, and the billing page
  • Cloudflare: our servers and database, email sending, site delivery and security checks
  • GitHub: hosts the website's pages
  • Resend: backup email delivery
  • Google: our business email inbox (Gmail), order texts (Google Voice), Google Analytics, YouTube videos, and fonts on some pages
  • Reddit: ad measurement (Reddit Pixel)
  • Anthropic: writes Ask K's answers
  • Discord: notifies our team about live support chats
  • Brevo: our newsletter list
  • Pirate Ship and USPS: shipping labels and delivery

We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect our customers or our business from fraud.

How long we keep information

  • Orders, bookings, invoices, payments and signed agreements: as long as we need them for business and tax records.
  • Contact form messages: in our email inbox until we delete them.
  • Live support chats: until we delete them.
  • Ask K chats: not saved. We keep only daily counts.
  • Request counts by IP address, and the one-way code from an order-update lookup: up to a day.
  • Server logs: about a week.
  • Visit counts: kept as statistics. They don't include IP addresses.

Your choices

  • Correction or deletion: to have information you've given us corrected or deleted, email contact@easternshore.ai. We'll handle it promptly, though we may need to keep some records, such as orders and payments, for legal, tax or operational reasons.
  • Texts: reply STOP, or tell us by phone or email.
  • Newsletter: use the unsubscribe link in any newsletter email, or ask us.
  • Cookies: block or delete them in your browser settings.

Children

Our site and products are meant for adults. Survival Node buyers must be 18 or older, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.
