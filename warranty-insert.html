Battery, Solar & Faraday Safety

Battery: Lithium-ion cells self-discharge — recharge the phone and solar battery every 6–12 months to prevent permanent capacity loss. Store cool and dry, 60–85°F (15–30°C); avoid cars, attics, garages. Do not puncture, crush, or wet batteries. Do not charge the solar battery and charge the phone from it at the same time. Dispose of damaged batteries per local regulations.

Faraday: EMI protection works only when the device is fully enclosed and the bag is folded over at least twice and sealed flat. Punctures, poor sealing, or material wear reduce or eliminate protection.

Solar: The panel is for emergency sustainment, not all-day use. In hot weather it should feel warm, not hot — move to shade if it overheats. Do not charge above 113°F (45°C).
