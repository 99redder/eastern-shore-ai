Battery, Solar & Faraday Bag Safety

Battery storage and maintenance

Lithium-ion batteries self-discharge over time. Recharge the phone and solar battery every 6–12 months to prevent permanent capacity loss. Store the kit in a cool, dry location — between 60°F (15°C) and 85°F (30°C). Avoid cars, attics, and garages where temperatures reach damaging extremes.

Do not puncture, crush, or expose batteries to moisture. Dispose of damaged batteries in accordance with local regulations. Do not charge the solar battery and use it to charge the phone at the same time.

Faraday bag integrity

Protection against electromagnetic interference is only effective when the device is fully enclosed and the bag is correctly folded and sealed. Punctures, improper sealing, or material degradation may reduce or eliminate protection. Always fold the top of the bag over at least twice and press the seal flat with no gaps.

Solar charging expectations

The solar panel is designed for emergency sustainment — not all-day normal use. Under emergency conditions (brief, task-focused use followed by powering the device fully off), solar alone can sustain the kit for an extended period. In hot weather, check the solar battery regularly; it should feel warm, not hot. Move it to shade if it overheats. Do not charge above 113°F (45°C).
