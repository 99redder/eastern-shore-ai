Capacity Verification

Rated capacity42,800 mAh (manufacturer provided, not tested)
Core Unit phone charges (full)3-4 full charges ✓
Result✓ Pass

The manufacturer rating of 42,800 mAh is a fairly large capacity for a solar battery. To put it in perspective: the Moto G Power's battery is approximately 5,000 mAh. That means this battery in theory can fully recharge the phone roughly 8–9 times from dead - I've personally tested this and found it to be closer to 3-4 times. But optimal battery storage practices are essential for maintaining your Survival Node over time. My routine is to charge to 60% after use (not 100%), which is considered by many to be the optimal battery storage level (source: TechFine - How to Store A Lithium Ion Battery) So while it is capable of probably fully charging more than 4 times, the actual number may vary based on usage patterns and environmental conditions and I don't believe that to be the best way to do it.
