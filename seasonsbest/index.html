Every booth is someone's own shop

With about 50 vendors under one roof, every booth reflects somebody's own taste. A few things regular shoppers point out:

  • Give yourself time

    Shoppers say it's easy to spend two hours or more looking around.

  • Check the back room

    One reviewer's tip: don't forget to browse it for hidden treasures.

  • Something new often

    Vendors update their booths all the time, so it pays to come back.

    A wheelchair-accessible lot and entrance, right on N Salisbury Blvd.
