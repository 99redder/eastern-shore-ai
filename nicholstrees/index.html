How it works

Picking out your tree

Come out during the season, walk the rows and pick the tree that fits your room. Here is what to know before you go.

1

Choose and cut

Over 20 acres and 16 varieties to walk through. You pick it, you cut it.

2

Bring a saw, or borrow ours

Bring your own saw if you like. If not, borrow one of ours.

3

One price, any size

Whatever size you choose, every tree is the same price. Call for this year's price.

4

Live balled trees

Want a tree you can plant after the holidays? Live balled trees are available too.

5

Twine for the ride home

We don't wrap trees. If you need it, we'll give you twine to tie yours down.

6

Cash only

We take cash only, so please bring cash with you.
